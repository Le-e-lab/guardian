## COMPETITIVE MOAT

### What Sentari Has That Others Don't

1. **African Threat Intelligence** — No one else has SIM-swap, USSD, mobile money fraud patterns
2. **Multi-Model AI Fusion** — 9 models across 4 providers, consensus scoring
3. **Price Point** — 100x cheaper than Western alternatives
4. **Data Sovereignty** — Data never leaves Africa by architecture
5. **Self-Service** — No sales calls needed, sign up and scan
6. **Compliance Templates** — POPIA, NDPA, Kenya DPA built-in

### What Competitors Could Copy

1. African threat data — takes 6-12 months to build
2. Multi-model fusion — technically possible but complex
3. Price point — requires African cost structure
4. Compliance templates — straightforward to replicate

### The Real Moat

**The African threat dataset is the moat.** Once you have:
- 10,000+ scans of African infrastructure
- Anonymized fraud patterns from design partners
- Regional CVE mapping
- Mobile money attack simulations

...no Western competitor can replicate this without years of on-the-ground work.
